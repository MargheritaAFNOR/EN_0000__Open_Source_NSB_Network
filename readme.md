Mission Statement — Open Source National Standardisation Bodies Network —  OSSENCE
==========

*OSSENCE* is an open-source initiative focused on advancing the use of open-source software and tools in the domain of standards development, interpretation, and collaboration. Its mission is to establish a transparent, interoperable, and community-driven framework that supports the full lifecycle of standards—from interpretation to authoring to cross-community integration.

Within its mission, *OSSENCE*:

* Enables the interpretation of standards through open, models, formalized semantics, and reproducible toolchains, reducing ambiguity and improving consistency across implementations.
* Facilitates the convergence of the open-source community and the standards community, promoting shared practices, open governance models, and bidirectional knowledge transfer.
* Defines interoperable interfaces and data formats to ensure compatibility between diverse tools, repositories, and organizational processes.
* Promotes transparency, auditability, and long-term accessibility of standards and their evolution.
* Supports extensibility to accommodate domain-specific requirements while preserving a stable and well-defined core architecture.


*OSSENCE* aims to lower barriers between specification and implementation by aligning open-source methodologies with formal standardization processes, creating a cohesive ecosystem for collaborative, high-quality standards development.
